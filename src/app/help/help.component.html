NLNG A & P Management Portal – Workflow & Help Guide

Welcome to the NLNG A & P Management portal – a web application used to manage operator and technician training assessments.

General Workflow
  1. Log in to the portal. The home page displays the navigation menu. Trainees primarily use Request Assessment, while assessors, supervisors and administrators access additional pages.
  2. Request an assessment. Open the Request Assessment tab to submit a new skills assessment request. Complete all mandatory fields and confirm your submission.
  3. SSI schedules the assessment. Evaluators use the SSI tab to review requests, schedule sessions and record results.
  4. Assessor conducts the assessment. The Assessor confirms the manning page, clicks Assess, conducts the physical assessment, uploads required documents, and selects the competency outcome.
  5. Head of Shift reviews. Once marked as competent, the assessment moves to the Head of Shift for review and approval.
  6. HRL4 and Verifier confirmation. HR staff confirm records in the HRL4 Assessment tab and verifiers perform a final check in Verifier.
  7. Batch management & progression. Use Batch Management to group assessments and send them for Plant Operations Management review. The Progression Letters tab generates letters for operators progressing to higher competency levels.
Role-Based Workflow Details
Operator / Trainee

Primary Tab: Request Assessment

Responsibilities:

  • Submit assessment requests for required skills/competencies
  • Complete all mandatory fields in the request form
  • Track assessment status through the portal
  • Attend scheduled assessment sessions
SSI (Skill-Specific Instructor)

Primary Tab: SSI

Responsibilities:

  • Review incoming assessment requests
  • Schedule assessment sessions with operators
  • Record assessment session details
  • Pass assessments to the Assessor for evaluation
Assessor

Primary Tab: Assessor

Responsibilities:

  • Confirm the manning page for the operator
  • Click Assess to begin the assessment process
  • Conduct the physical/practical assessment
  • Upload assessment documents (required before submission)
  • Select competency outcome:
Competent

Assessment moves forward to Head of Shift for review

Not Competent

Assessment remains with the Assessor. The operator must return for re-assessment. Use the Edit button to update the assessment when the operator becomes competent.

Needs Improvement

Assessment requires additional work before competency can be confirmed

Important: You must upload assessment documents before submitting. An error will occur if you attempt to submit without uploading documents.
Head of Shift

Primary Tab: Head of Shift

Responsibilities:

  • Review assessments marked as Competent by Assessors
  • Verify assessment documentation is complete
  • Approve or return assessments for correction
  • Forward approved assessments to HRL4
HRL4 (HR Level 4)

Primary Tab: HRL4 Assessment

Responsibilities:

  • Confirm completion of assessment documentation
  • Verify HR records are updated
  • Process assessments for final verification
Verifier

Primary Tab: Verifier

Responsibilities:

  • Conduct final quality check of assessment records
  • Verify all documentation is complete and accurate
  • Confirm assessments for batch processing
Administrator

Primary Tabs: Admin submenu, Batch Management

Responsibilities:

  • Manage plant areas, blocks, units, and shifts
  • Configure assessment templates
  • Manage user accounts and assign roles
  • Define progression levels and requirements
  • Group assessments into batches for POM review
  • Generate progression letters
